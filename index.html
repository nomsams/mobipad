A little nudge.

01 · Find a groove. Tap a pad in Loops mode. The next one joins on the bar. One shots play immediately.

02 · Add your voice. Record the microphone or import audio. Double-tap a populated pad to edit. Empty pads open their editor.

03 · Play it in. Pick an instrument and an octave. Capture starts on the next beat, with optional count-ins. Played notes snap to sixteenths; arpeggios keep their exact rhythm.

04 · Build a band. Create a room and share its QR. The leader controls tempo, meter, and transport. Other phones follow. Use Listen & align on a follower near the leader's speaker.

Keys. Space: play/pause · 1–4, Q–R, A–F, Z–V: pads · A–K: piano in Instruments · Esc: stop.

Good to know. Imported loops use pitch-preserving time stretching. BPM and key suggestions are estimates. Use headphones for recording, wired audio for lower latency, and keep the app in the foreground. Piano, bass, and guitar are synthesized instruments.

Follow this sample’s groove?

All loop audio will be time-stretched to this tempo while preserving pitch. Recorded instrument notes keep their pitch. Very large tempo changes can introduce stretching artifacts.
